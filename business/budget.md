> OIO's annual budget overview. What it costs to run the channel and race.

*Last updated: March 2026*

## Budget Philosophy

OIO is a bootstrap operation. Every dollar spent is weighed against what it enables — racing, content, or channel growth. Sponsorships are sought specifically to offset racing and production costs.

**The goal:** Sponsorships cover costs so racing and content continue regardless of channel revenue.
