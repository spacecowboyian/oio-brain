## What Is OIO

**Outside Inside Outside Racing** — Ian Jennings' grassroots motorsports brand and YouTube channel out of Kansas City, MO. Autocross, rallycross, car builds, race prep, and DIY content that makes motorsports feel accessible to regular people.

- **Channel:** @oioracing
- **Subscribers:** 1.93K | **Videos:** 358
- **Sign-off:** *"Until next time, remember to go out and find your own apex, because it's better late than never."*

## Standing Rules (Never Break These)

- **One Honda Fit How-To per month, every month, forever.**
- **Rallycross and autocross are NOT the same.** Never flatten them together.
- **Church of Combustion episodes drop on Sunday.**
- **Ian is always the butt of the joke. The cars humble him.**
- **6-hour edit cap** on How-Tos and vlogs. **12–15 hours** on Church of Combustion.
