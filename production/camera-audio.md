# Camera and Audio Workflow

> How OIO captures footage. Equipment, setup, and field workflow for shoots.

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## Production Reality

Ian shoots everything solo. There is no crew. Occasionally family and friends shoot iPhone footage that gets used. All production decisions are made under this constraint.

---

## Equipment

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| 360 camera | Immersive event coverage, in-car angles, garage wide shots |
| DJI audio gear | Mic for direct-to-camera narration, interview, garage voiceover |
| Phone(s) | B-roll, backup footage, social clips |
| GoPro / action camera | In-car, under-car, mounting where 360 doesn't fit |
| iPhone (family/friends) | Event day footage — acceptable quality for vlog and COC |

---

## Shot Types by Format

### Honda Fit How-To
- Direct-to-camera narration (kids doing the work, Ian supervising)
- Close-up of the task being performed
- Before / after
- Clean audio is essential — use DJI mic

**Framing priority:** Show the work. The hands on the part. The torque wrench on the bolt.

### Vlog
- Opens at the Project Board — wide shot of the board, medium on Ian
- Shop/garage B-roll throughout
- Direct-to-camera updates
- Process shots (whatever work is happening)
- Closes at the Project Board

**Framing priority:** Feel of being in the garage. Not too polished.

### Church of Combustion
- Event run footage — best angle available
- In-car footage
- Reaction / crowd footage
- Scripted direct-to-camera segments (sepia-toned aesthetic)
- Ian in flat cap and checkered tie for Reverend segments
- Ian holding *Sportsmanlike Driving* for Reverend segments

**Framing priority:** Cinematic where possible. The 360 camera earns its keep here.

### Shorts / Social
- Cut from existing footage — rarely shot specifically as a Short
- Hook moment in first 2 seconds
- Vertical format or square

## Pre-Shoot Checklist

- [ ] All cameras charged
- [ ] SD cards cleared and formatted
- [ ] Audio gear charged and tested
- [ ] Mounts and accessories packed
- [ ] In-car mount installed and secure
- [ ] Backup phone charged

---

## Field Workflow

### At an Event
1. 360 camera on car before grid
2. DJI mic on Ian for paddock commentary
3. Phone with helper for outside shots during runs
4. Collect all footage to a single device or drive at end of day
5. Back up immediately — don't sleep on raw footage

### In the Garage
1. Set up DJI mic before starting work
2. Hit record before starting — get natural moments
3. Close-ups of parts, tools, and problem areas
4. Don't stage if you can capture real

---

## File Organization

- Dump all footage to a dated folder: `YYYY-MM-DD-[Shoot Name]`
- Keep raw files — don't delete until video is published and backed up
- Label which clips are 360, which are phone, which are DJI
