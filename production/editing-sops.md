# Editing SOPs

> Standard operating procedures for editing OIO videos. Follow these to stay within time budgets and maintain quality.

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## Edit Time Budgets

| Format | Edit Budget | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Honda Fit How-To | 6 hours max | Exceed this and stop — ship it |
| Vlog | 6 hours max | Same rule |
| Church of Combustion | 12–15 hours | The big production — worth the time |
| Short | 1 hour max | It's a clip. Cut it, caption it, post it. |

**The 6-hour rule exists for a reason.** Perfectionism kills the content schedule. Ship the video.

---

## Honda Fit How-To — Edit SOP

**Goal:** Clear, practical, watchable. The viewer should be able to do the task after watching.

1. **Assembly cut** — rough assembly of all usable shots in order (~1 hour)
2. **Narration pass** — trim dead air, fix pacing (~1.5 hours)
3. **Graphics/text** — add any annotations, on-screen specs, part numbers (~1 hour)
4. **Audio pass** — level the DJI mic, cut noise, add music bed if needed (~45 min)
5. **Final watch** — one full playback, fix only critical issues (~45 min)
6. **Export and upload**

**Music:** Royalty-free bed. Low volume. Doesn't compete with narration.

---

## Vlog — Edit SOP

**Goal:** Feels like being in the garage with Ian. Natural energy, story moves forward.

1. **Story beat outline** — what happens in this episode? List 3–5 beats before touching footage
2. **Assembly cut** — pull the shots that hit the beats (~1.5 hours)
3. **Project Board open and close** — must be in every vlog. Find the cleanest shots of both.
4. **Pacing pass** — cut dead time, tighten (~1.5 hours)
5. **Audio pass** — levels, music, ambient sound (~1 hour)
6. **Final watch + export** (~1 hour)

---

## Church of Combustion — Edit SOP

**Goal:** Cinematic, scripted, feels like a religious experience. The premium OIO format.

1. **Script review** — confirm the structure before touching footage
2. **Reverend segments** — cut and grade first (sepia tone, tight framing)
3. **Event footage assembly** — best runs, best moments, ordered to match script
4. **Narration sync** — Reverend voiceover over footage where applicable
5. **Music pass** — Church of Combustion has a distinct sound. Match the tone.
6. **Color grade** — sepia for Reverend, normal for event, consistent within each
7. **Graphics pass** — title cards, results, congregation call-outs
8. **Audio master** — every element balanced
9. **Final watch** — full playback, church-quality check
10. **Export and upload — SUNDAY ONLY**

## Export Settings (YouTube)

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Resolution | 1080p minimum, 4K if footage allows |
| Frame rate | Match shoot frame rate |
| Format | H.264 or H.265 |
| Audio | AAC, 320kbps or higher |
