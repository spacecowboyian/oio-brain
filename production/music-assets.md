## Music Policy

All music used in OIO videos must be either:
- **Royalty-free / license purchased** — safe for YouTube monetization
- **Creative Commons** — with correct attribution in description
- **Original / commissioned** — fully owned

**Never use:**
- Commercial music without a sync license
- Music from YouTube's free library that has Content ID — it will trigger claims
