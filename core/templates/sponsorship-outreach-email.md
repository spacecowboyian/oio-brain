# Template — Sponsorship Outreach Email

> Copy this and fill in the brackets. Keep it short. Sponsors get a lot of pitches.

---

```
Subject: Sponsorship opportunity — OIO Racing (@oioracing)

Hi [Name],

I'm Ian Jennings, creator of OIO Racing — a grassroots motorsports YouTube channel out of Kansas City that focuses on autocross, rallycross, and project-car builds.

I'm reaching out because [specific reason this company fits — be specific, not generic].

Quick stats:
- 1.93K subscribers, 358 videos
- Top video: 42K views (Honda Fit how-to)
- Audience: real car owners who buy parts and enter events

My Honda Fit how-to content in particular drives consistent search traffic from people actively working on their cars — which is where [Company]'s products would be a natural fit.

I'd love to discuss what a partnership could look like — whether that's a product feature, sponsored segment, or something else that makes sense for you.

Are you open to a quick conversation?

Thanks,
Ian Jennings
OIO Racing
YouTube: @oioracing
[Contact info]
```

---

## Notes on Using This Template

- **Be specific in paragraph 2.** Generic pitches go in the trash. Tell them exactly why their product fits OIO.
- Keep the stats brief — one line each.
- Don't over-promise reach. Be honest about where the channel is.
- The Fit How-To angle is the strongest hook — lean on it for parts/tools companies.
- Follow up once after 1 week if no response. After that, move on.
