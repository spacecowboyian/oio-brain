# Mission and Positioning

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## Mission Statement

**Make motorsports feel accessible to regular people.**

OIO Racing documents the real experience of grassroots motorsports — the wrenching, the spending, the failing, the winning — so that anyone watching can see themselves doing it too.

---

## Brand Positioning

**Outside Inside Outside Racing** is the grassroots motorsports brand for people who thought they couldn't do it.

OIO occupies the space between:
- Too technical (full race prep content for serious competitors)
- Too casual (lifestyle vlogging with no substance)

OIO is **practical comedy with racing underneath it**.

---

## One-Line Description

> OIO is a grassroots motorsports brand built around practical car prep, budget-minded racing, project-car storytelling, and making autocross and rallycross feel accessible to regular people.

---

## The Brand Promise

A stranger can land on any OIO video, feel the world immediately, and want to stay.

That world is:
- Funny without being stupid
- Real without being depressing
- Technical without being gatekeeping
- Personal without being self-indulgent

---

## Competitive Positioning

| Type of Channel | What They Do | Where OIO Differs |
|---|---|---|
| Technical motorsport | Deep dives on setup and data | OIO keeps it human and funny |
| Car build vlogs | Long-form builds, big budgets | OIO is budget-constrained and honest about it |
| Comedy car content | Broad comedy, thin substance | OIO has real racing outcomes at stake |
| Vice Grip Garage | Folk hero mechanic energy | OIO adds the Church bit and racing outcomes |

---

## What OIO Is Explicitly Not

- Not corporate marketing
- Not influencer fluff
- Not a technical breakdown channel for hardcore fans
- Not aspirational "I have a sponsor-funded build" content
- Not a review channel

---

## Long-Term Vision

- 10K+ subscribers with a tight, loyal congregation
- Videos consistently hitting 2–5K views
- Sponsorships covering operational costs
- The kids competing as documented racers
- Lake Garnett Grand Prix Revival as the annual marquee event
- OIO as the brand that made someone sign up for their first autocross

---

## The Tagline

> *"Until next time, remember to go out and find your own apex, because it's better late than never."*
