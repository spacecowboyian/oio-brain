## The Ask

OIO Racing is looking for sponsorship partners to help cover the real costs of grassroots motorsport content — fuel, tires, entry fees, parts, and production gear.

**In return:** Authentic integration into a growing, loyal audience that trusts OIO's recommendations because they see us actually use the products on the cars.

## Why OIO

**The audience is real and earned.**
Every subscriber came through genuine content — not paid promotion, not virality. The Honda Fit how-to funnel attracts real car owners with practical needs. They stay for the story.

**The audience is action-oriented.**
OIO viewers buy parts, enter events, and work on their own cars. They act on recommendations.

**The channel is growing.**
Fit how-to content dramatically outperforms everything else in raw views. The top-of-funnel is working. Subscriber conversion is the current growth challenge — not audience interest.

**The content is evergreen.**
Maintenance how-tos have long search lifespans. A product featured in a Fit How-To gets discovered for years.

## Sponsorship Tiers

*Tiers are flexible. Reach out to discuss what makes sense.*

### Presenting Sponsor
- Named in channel intro/outro
- Product featured in dedicated video(s)
- Integration in relevant how-to and vlog content
- Logo placement on featured cars (events)
- Social shoutouts

### Supporting Sponsor
- Product featured in relevant content
- Verbal mention in related episodes
- Social shoutouts

### Product Partner
- Product provided, featured organically when relevant
- Credit in video description
