# Audience Personas

> Who watches OIO, why they watch, and how to talk to them.

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## How the Audience Finds OIO

**The funnel:**
1. Search a Honda Fit maintenance question on YouTube
2. Find a practical, well-made How-To with kids doing the work
3. Notice it's funny
4. Fall into the comedy universe — the Church, the Goblin's suffering, the kids
5. Subscribe and become congregation

The Honda Fit How-To videos are the top-of-funnel engine. Everything else is the world they stay for.

---

## Primary Persona — The Relatable Wrencher

**Who they are:**
- 15–45 years old
- Owns a practical car (or wants to)
- Curious about motorsports but hasn't tried it (or just started)
- Watches YouTube for both utility and entertainment
- Appreciates humor that doesn't insult their intelligence

**What they want:**
- Practical info they can actually use
- To feel like motorsports is something they could do
- Entertainment alongside the substance
- To see real people with real budgets doing real things

**What they fear:**
- Looking dumb in front of car people
- Spending money on the wrong thing
- Getting in over their head

**How OIO speaks to them:**
- Show the mistakes, not just the wins
- Keep the cost conversation honest
- Ian is always the butt of the joke so they don't have to be
- The Church wraps it in enough absurdity that nothing feels precious

---

## Secondary Persona — The Comedy Fan Who Ended Up Here

**Who they are:**
- Not primarily a car person
- Stumbled onto OIO through algorithm or social sharing
- Stayed because the bit is committed and consistent
- Might not own a modified car or race

**What they want:**
- Consistent entertainment with a developed universe
- Characters they can follow across episodes
- Payoffs to running jokes (the Goblin never works, the Project Board is overwhelming)

**What they fear:**
- Getting lost if they don't know about cars
- Content that assumes too much knowledge

**How OIO speaks to them:**
- Explain things naturally, without condescension
- Make the stakes clear even without car knowledge (Goblin might not make it to the event)
- Keep the Church of Combustion bit layered enough to reward return viewers

---

## Tertiary Persona — The Grassroots Motorsport Regular

**Who they are:**
- Already autocrosses or rallycrosses
- Knows what a cone is, knows what understeer feels like
- Looking for community and entertainment, not tutorials

**What they want:**
- Recognition of the shared experience
- Content that doesn't dumb things down for them
- Humor that lands because they've been there

**What they fear:**
- Being talked down to
- Content that oversimplifies their sport

**How OIO speaks to them:**
- Don't dumb down the motorsport content — they'll catch everything
- The "things rallycross drivers say" bucket is made for them
- Church of Combustion recap format is highly watchable because they lived the same events

---

## What the Audience Is NOT

- Hardcore technical fans looking for data analysis and lap time optimization
- Sponsorship-chasing influencer audiences
- People who care more about the car's value than its story
