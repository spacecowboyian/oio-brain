# SCCA E Street Touring (EST) — 2026 Rules Reference

**Source:** 2026 SCCA National Solo Rulebook, February edition
**Section:** 14 (Street Touring Category)
**Class:** E Street Touring (EST)
**Last verified:** April 13, 2026

---

## What EST Is

E Street Touring. 2026 rename of what was previously STF. Street Touring Category, sub-letter E. Class description from rulebook:

> "E Street Touring (EST) – Primarily low-power sedans, coupes, hatchbacks and older higher performing RWD and FWD cars"

This is the Honda Fit's home class. Also Yaris, non-Si Civic, Corolla, Scion xA/xB/xD/tC, Matrix, Versa, non-turbo Mazda3, Mazda2, Protegé, Miata (1990–97 non-Torsen), MINI Cooper (non-S), Pontiac Vibe, Saturn economy cars, Scion iA/iM/iQ, non-turbo Impreza, VW Beetle 2.5/8v, Golf/Jetta 8v and TDI, Golf/Rabbit/GTI/Cabrio 1974–92, Toyota Celica non-turbo 1986–2005, non-turbo MR2, Echo, and the full "NOC" catch-all for sub-3.1L NA coupes and sedans.

## Tires & Wheels (Sections 14.3, 14.4)

- **Max tire section width:** 225mm
- **Max wheel width:** 7.5"

This is narrower than most other Street Touring subclasses. DST (AWD) allows 245 / 8.0", GST (AWD) allows 245 / 9.0", etc.

## Engine Allowances (Section 14.10)

### ECU & Tuning

- Reprogrammable without restriction (14.10.F)
- TCU reprogrammable for transmission behavior only (14.10.P)
- Fuel pressure regulators replaceable in lieu of electronic alterations
- Emissions compliance still required

### Intake

- Free up to engine inlet (throttle body, carb, compressor inlet, or intake manifold, whichever comes first)
- MAF / PCV valve / emissions components in flow path must remain functional
- Cannot modify car structure to route ducting

### Exhaust

- Headers, downpipes, EGR tubes all replaceable
- Any catalytic converters allowed
- Exit may be relocated (within Section 3.3.3 safety requirements)
- Exhaust heat shields on replaced parts may be modified/removed

### Engine Rebuild Allowances (14.10.R)

- **Overbore: up to 0.020" (0.508 mm) maximum**
- Sleeving allowed to repair to standard bore
- **OE-type pistons only**, same configuration, same or greater weight
- **No cast-to-forged swap**
- Standard overbore increments recommended

### What's NOT Allowed for Engines

- No alternate cams or regrinds
- No port work, no port matching
- No alternate intake manifold
- No bored throttle body
- No lightweight flywheel
- No forced induction added
- No alternate pistons beyond OE-type oversize
- No head work beyond factory service manual deck spec

### Engine/Trans Mounts (14.10.J)

- Any mount legal, provided:
  - Original mounting points only
  - No engine/trans relocation (other than incidental compliance material changes)
  - Weight ≥ OE mount weight
- Components between engine and mounting structure are part of the mount assembly

### Clutch & Flywheel

- **Clutch disk and pressure plate may be modified or replaced** (14.10.O)
- **Flywheel must remain OE weight** — this is the accidental DQ trap. Many clutch kits ship with lightweight flywheels by default. Spec the OE-weight version or resurface the original.

## Limited Slip Differentials (14.10.K) — THE BIG RESTRICTION

**EST is the only Street Touring subclass that bans aftermarket LSDs.** Verbatim from the rulebook:

> **K. Limited Slip Differentials**
>
> 1. **EST class:** No limited slip differentials are permitted except for factory standard viscous coupler-type units.
> 1. SST, AST, BST, CST, and DST classes: 2WD vehicles may use any mechanical LSD unit. AWD vehicles may substitute one differential (front, rear, or center) with an aftermarket mechanical LSD.
> 1. GST: 2WD vehicles may use any mechanical LSD unit.

### What Counts as a "Factory Standard Viscous Coupler-Type Unit"

Very few cars on the EST FWD list qualify. The rule is effectively a grandfather clause for:

- **Nissan Sentra SE-R / NX2000 (1991–94, B13 chassis)** — factory viscous LSD in SE-R trim
- **Nissan 200SX SE-R (1995–98, B14 chassis)** — factory viscous LSD in some trims

The B13 SE-R is almost certainly the car the rule was written around.

### What Does NOT Qualify

- Helical / Torsen LSDs (Mazda Protegé MP3, Neon ACR with Quaife, etc.) — these are mechanical, not viscous
- Any aftermarket LSD of any type, in any car
- Factory LSDs that are clutch-type or mechanical rather than viscous

### Cars on the EST FWD List That Did NOT Come With Any Factory LSD

Honda Fit, Honda non-Si Civic, Honda Accord 4-cyl, Honda CRX, Honda CR-Z, Honda del Sol, Honda Insight, Toyota Yaris, Toyota Corolla non-GR, Toyota Echo, Toyota Matrix, Scion xA/xB/xD/tC/iA/iM/iQ, Mazda 323/Mazda2/Mazda3 non-turbo/Mazda6 non-turbo, Ford Fiesta non-turbo, Ford Focus non-turbo, Chevy Cobalt/Cruze/Sonic/Spark, Dodge Neon (most), Hyundai Accent/Elantra/Veloster non-turbo, Kia Forte/Rio, VW Beetle/Golf/Jetta 8v, VW Golf/Rabbit/GTI/Cabrio 1974–92, Nissan Sentra 1.6/1.8/2.0, Nissan Versa, Pontiac Vibe, Saturn Ion/SL/SW/SC, Subaru Impreza FWD, Toyota Celica non-turbo, Toyota MR2 non-turbo.

**Bottom line:** For virtually every car in the EST FWD field, no legal LSD exists. The class is run open-diff.

## Suspension (Sections 14.5, 14.6, 14.7, 14.8)

### Shocks

- Any shock absorbers allowed
- Must attach to original mounting points
- Same number as stock
- No in-motion adjustment unless OE

### Springs

- Change or modification of springs, coil spring perches, torsion bar levers, leaf spring shackles allowed
- Ride height changes via adjustment only (no hub-carrier spacers etc.)

### Camber Plates

- Allowed on strut-type suspensions
- Original upper mounting holes may be slotted
- Drilling for installation permitted
- Alternate upper spring perch / mounting block allowed
- Any resulting caster change permitted

### Camber Bolts

- Allowed, using original unmodified mounting points

### Anti-Roll Bars

- Substitution, addition, or removal permitted
- Bushing material, attachment method, locating points unrestricted
- Cannot cut holes to route bars or links

### Camber Kits

- Allowed on multi-link / non-strut rears
- Lateral adjustment to effective control arm length

### Torsion Beam Rears (like Honda Fit)

Very limited options. Alignment shim kits (Whiteline, Ingalls) work within the bushing but meaningful rear camber requires axle modification which exits the class.

## Brakes

- Non-standard rotors allowed, equal or larger dimensions, ferrous material
- Aluminum rotor hats allowed
- Cross-drilled/slotted rotors permitted if voids are within disc area and <10% of that area
- Calipers and brackets may be replaced if bolt-on at standard locations with equal-or-greater piston count
- Brake lines: alternate DOT-approved flexible lines allowed
- Drum-to-disc conversions allowed with conditions (bolted not welded, integral e-brake)
- ABS may be electronically disabled but otherwise must remain unmodified

## EST Philosophy

Per the rulebook category description:

> "The Street Touring® category of vehicle modifications is meant to fit between the current Street and Street Prepared categories. This category provides a natural competition outlet for auto enthusiasts using affordable… vehicles with bolt-on modifications."

Core principle: **bolt-on only**. Modifications should not require cutting, drilling, or permanent alterations to body panels. Vehicle must remain street-legal and daily drivable in principle.

EST specifically is the class for slower / older / lower-power cars. It's the "cheap to race, hard to cheat" class. The LSD ban and the tight tire width are both deliberate choices to keep the class accessible and keep budgets down.

## Common Accidental DQ Traps

1. **Lightweight flywheel.** Most common. Clutch kits ship with them by default. Always spec OE-weight.
2. **Alternate LSD.** Even a factory-type LSD from a different trim may not count if it's not the correct viscous type.
3. **Overbore past 0.020".** Street Prepared allows 0.047". EST does not.
4. **Forged pistons.** Even if same displacement and weight, the cast-to-forged swap is explicitly banned.
5. **Port-matched head.** Common mod for NA builds. Illegal in ST.
6. **225+ tires.** 225 is the LIMIT, not the target. Some 225-labeled tires measure wider and could get protested.
