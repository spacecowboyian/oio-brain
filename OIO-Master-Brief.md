# OIO Racing — Master Brief
*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## What Is OIO

**Outside Inside Outside Racing** is Ian Jennings' grassroots motorsports brand and YouTube channel out of Kansas City, MO. Focused on autocross, rallycross, car builds, race prep, and automotive DIY content — aimed at making motorsports feel accessible to regular people.

- **Channel:** @oioracing
- **Subscribers:** 1.93K | **Videos:** 358
- **Sign-off line:** *"Until next time, remember to go out and find your own apex, because it's better late than never."*
- **One-line description:** OIO is a grassroots motorsports brand built around practical car prep, budget-minded racing, project-car storytelling, and making autocross and rallycross feel accessible to regular people.

---

## Tone & Brand Voice

Lonely Island meets Vice Grip Garage. Absurdist comedy commitment meets folksy Midwest "will it run" drama. Ian is always the butt of the joke. The cars humble him. He documents it on purpose.

- **Target audience:** Comedy-first people who happen to like cars — not hardcore motorsport fans looking for technical breakdowns.
- **Brand tone:** Practical, enthusiastic, hands-on, mildly irreverent, encouraging, grounded in real effort and real constraints.
- **Feel:** Knowledgeable garage talk. Not corporate marketing. Not influencer fluff.

---

## The Church of Combustion

The entire channel is wrapped in a bit: OIO operates as a Southern Baptist-style church where combustion engines are worshipped and racing is an act of faith.

- Ian plays **The Reverend**
- Racing events = Sunday services
- **Lake Garnett Grand Prix Revival** = the annual revival meeting / the cathedral moment of the season
- Visual language: sepia-toned, Ian in a flat cap and checkered tie, holding *Sportsmanlike Driving* — a vintage AAA driver's ed manual repurposed as the church's sacred scripture
- Church of Combustion episodes always drop on **Sunday**

---

## Message Pillars

**Access** — Motorsports is more accessible than people think.

**Learn by doing** — You don't need to know everything to get started.

**Prep matters** — Reliability, maintenance, and smart setup beat fantasy mod lists.

**Small cars can be heroes** — Cars like the Honda Fit, old Toyotas, and other underdogs deserve attention.

**Build the space, build the habit** — A better garage and better workflow make more racing and better projects possible.

**Family / team / community** — Grassroots motorsports is more fun when it includes the people around you.

## Content System

Four rotating weekly formats:

| Week | Format | Drop Day | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Honda Fit How-To | Thursday | SEO-optimized maintenance. Top-of-funnel growth engine. Kids do the work. 6-hour edit cap. |
| Week 2 | Vlog | Thursday | Garage work, build progress, race prep. Opens and closes at the project board. 6-hour cap. |
| Week 3 | Church of Combustion | Sunday | Scripted racing event recap. Big production. 12–15 hours. Drops 1–2 weeks post-event. |
| Week 4 | Vlog | Thursday | Build progress, congregation updates, new arcs opening. |

**Special formats:**
- *Sunday School* — technique and theory, delivered by people absolutely not qualified to teach it
- *FIT OFF* — annual Honda Fit battle event
- *The 2001 Car* — Ryan's Gambler Camry shorts

**Important rule:** Rallycross and autocross are NOT the same. Different tire needs, setup priorities, driving demands, and prep decisions. Never flatten them together.

---

## The Project Board

Ian runs a physical wall-mounted project management system in the garage — vertical strings for each month (March–October), color-coded index cards per car per task. Opens and closes every vlog episode. Doubles as a stained glass window visual gag in Church of Combustion episodes. The sheer volume of cards is its own punchline.

## The Honda Fit Is The Secret Weapon

Fit how-to content dramatically outperforms everything else in raw views. The community finds OIO through practical maintenance videos, then falls into the comedy universe.

**Standing rule: one Fit How-To per month, every month, forever.**

*"The Honda Fit is the next Miata of grassroots motorsports. It hauls gear like a truck, corners like a nervous housecat, and attacks a rallycross course with the determination of a caffeinated squirrel."*

Top performers:
- GD3 Armrest Hack — 42K views
- Valve Adjustment — 27K views
- Battery Swap — 13K views
- Throttle Body Upgrade — 11K views
- Rear Drum Brakes — 8.7K views

## Content Buckets

1. **Beginner education** — How to get started in rallycross/autocross, gear you actually need, pre/post-race maintenance, common beginner mistakes, cheapest upgrades that matter
2. **Vehicle-specific build & prep** — Honda Fit mods, MR2 maintenance, Celica build, suspension/tires/alignment
3. **Garage & workshop** — Organizing, tool storage, workspace improvements, budget upgrades
4. **Comparison & explainer** — Daily driver vs race car, autocross vs rallycross, tire/suspension comparisons
5. **Funny / relatable** — Things rallycross drivers say, every car guy ever, 5 signs you're addicted to grassroots racing
6. **Creator/process** — How Ian writes, films, edits; gear and workflow; balancing projects and racing
7. **Team & community** — Congregation car features, challenge content, local motorsports spotlights

## Production Approach

Ian shoots, edits, posts, and promotes everything solo. Mix of:
- Direct-to-camera garage/shop narration
- Event footage
- In-car footage
- Outside action shots
- Family/friend-shot iPhone footage
- 360 camera angles
- Lower-polish but high-energy editing where appropriate

**Tools:** 360 cameras, DJI audio gear, phone footage from helpers/family, scripting for educational videos, casual capture for garage/vlog content.
