## Content Cadence — The Four-Week Rotation

| Week | Format | Drop Day | Edit Budget | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Honda Fit How-To | Thursday | 6 hours | SEO-optimized maintenance. Top-of-funnel growth engine. Kids do the work. |
| Week 2 | Vlog | Thursday | 6 hours | Garage work, build progress, race prep. Opens and closes at the Project Board. |

## The Project Board

Ian runs a physical wall-mounted project management system in the garage:

- **Format:** Vertical strings per month (March–October), color-coded index cards per car per task
- **Function:** Opens and closes every vlog episode
- **Visual gag:** Doubles as a stained glass window in Church of Combustion episodes
- **The joke:** The sheer volume of cards is its own punchline

Every vlog starts and ends at the Project Board. It is the throughline.

## Content Bucket Rotation

Use these buckets to ensure variety across the calendar:

1. **Beginner education** — How to get started in rallycross/autocross, gear you actually need, pre/post-race maintenance, common beginner mistakes, cheapest upgrades that matter
2. **Vehicle-specific build & prep** — Honda Fit mods, MR2 maintenance, Celica build, suspension/tires/alignment
3. **Garage & workshop** — Organizing, tool storage, workspace improvements, budget upgrades
4. **Comparison & explainer** — Daily driver vs race car, autocross vs rallycross, tire/suspension comparisons
5. **Funny / relatable** — Things rallycross drivers say, every car guy ever, 5 signs you're addicted to grassroots racing
6. **Creator/process** — How Ian writes, films, edits; gear and workflow; balancing projects and racing
7. **Team & community** — Congregation car features, challenge content, local motorsports spotlights

## Standing Rules — Never Break These

- **One Honda Fit How-To per month, every month, forever.**
- **Rallycross and autocross are NOT the same.** Different tire needs, setup priorities, driving demands, prep decisions. Never flatten them together.
- **Church of Combustion episodes drop on Sunday.**
- **Ian is always the butt of the joke. The cars humble him. He documents it on purpose.**
- **6-hour edit cap** on How-Tos and Vlogs.
- **12–15 hour edit budget** for Church of Combustion.
- **Church of Combustion drops 1–2 weeks after the event** — never same-day.
- **Capacity realism rule:** default plans to one major hands-on execution track at a time unless additional owners and time blocks are explicitly assigned.

## Production Approach

Ian shoots, edits, posts, and promotes everything solo.

**Shot types used:**
- Direct-to-camera garage/shop narration
- Event footage
- In-car footage
- Outside action shots
- Family/friend-shot iPhone footage
- 360 camera angles
- Lower-polish but high-energy editing where appropriate

**Tools:**
- 360 cameras
- DJI audio gear
- Phone footage from helpers/family
- Scripted for educational videos
- Casual capture for garage/vlog content
