## Fabrication Notes

### Custom Coolant Pipe — 4A-FE Swap (April 2026)

Keegan fabricated a custom steel coolant pipe to route coolant properly with the 4A-FE swap. The factory routing doesn't translate when swapping engines the car was never designed for, so this piece was welded up from scratch.

**Photo 1 — pipe on the bench:** The finished pipe sitting on a vise in the shop. Straight main tube with a welded 90-degree stub on one end and a straight outlet on the other. Both ends show fresh MIG welds. Painted/coated black. Shop context: Prestone jug, cardboard boxes, typical garage chaos in the background.

**Photo 2 — pipe installed in engine bay:** The pipe fitted into the Tercel's engine bay next to the red valve cover (4A-FE). The lower end connects to an aluminum stub with a visible weld bead. White engine bay walls, wiring runs alongside. Tight fit — exactly why this piece had to be custom.
