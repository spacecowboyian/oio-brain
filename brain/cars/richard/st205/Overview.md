## Background

Richard's ST205 is arguably the most exotic car to regularly appear on the OIO channel. A JDM, right-hand-drive, AWD Toyota Celica GT-Four — the kind of car that gets a Church of Combustion episode written about it.

The car was a rumored marketplace deal for some time before Richard actually acquired it. When it arrived, the Church of Combustion covered it in the December 2025 episode: *"The Celica ST205 Has Been Called. The Dirt Will Judge."* One week later, Richard had already raced it in the dirt.

Richard is a serious SCCA competitor based in Manhattan, KS. The ST205 is his primary rallycross car.
