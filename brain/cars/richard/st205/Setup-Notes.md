## Notes on the ST205

The ST205 is the 1994–1999 generation Celica GT-Four. It uses a 3S-GTE turbocharged engine, full-time AWD, and was built for the World Rally Championship homologation. Richard's is a JDM import — right-hand drive.

This is a sophisticated, high-performance car by any standard. Its presence at a KSSCCA rallycross event is inherently interesting.

---

## Rallycross Considerations

- AWD is an advantage on loose surfaces
- Turbocharged power delivery requires attention to turbo heat management
- RHD import — parts sourcing may be JDM-specific in some cases
