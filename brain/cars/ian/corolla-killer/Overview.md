## Background

A 1977 Toyota Corolla Sport Coupe loaded with vintage TRD parts. Dropped a valve mid-autocross approximately five years ago. Has sat since. The current plan involves a $100 mystery motor and a very long list of TRD components.

The resurrection arc is one of the longest-running stories in OIO history. The car has been down for six years. 2026 is when it starts coming back.

---

## The $100 Mystery Motor

Ian acquired a mystery motor for $100. Whether it runs, what exactly it is, and whether it belongs in this car are all open questions. This is documented content.

## TRD Parts Collection

The Killer Corolla is loaded with vintage TRD (Toyota Racing Development) parts. Part of the appeal is that it's a period-correct Toyota race car, not a modified street car.
