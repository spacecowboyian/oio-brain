## Setup Philosophy

Dale is built for paved track use — autocross and eventually time attack. Setup priorities:
- Precision over durability (opposite of Goblin)
- Low roll, accurate turn-in
- Alignment dialed for the surface
- Tires appropriate for pavement grip
- Being developed as a real performance car, not just a vintage cruiser

## Brakes & Pedal

### Brake Pedal

**Completed:**
- New rubber brake pedal cover installed

This sounds minor, but it signals the kind of practical, tactile prep Ian does before events. On a vintage competition car, a pedal surface matters.

### Brake Bias

**Status:** Needs adjustment

After the first autocross testing, Ian identified brake bias needed adjustment.

That suggests the braking system was usable but not fully balanced for performance driving.

**Possible Implications:**
- Rear/front balance not ideal
- Confidence under braking was compromised
- Car rotation or stability under threshold braking may not have been right
