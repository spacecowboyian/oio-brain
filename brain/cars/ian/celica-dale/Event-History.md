## Major Events

**Event Significance:**
- Ian's favorite autocross event of the year
- Happens at the end of October
- They set up the same course every year
- Provides year-over-year comparison opportunities

#### 2023 Halloweenie

**Performance Benchmark:**
- Time: around 54 seconds (last year before motor swap)

This gives Dale a concrete before/after benchmark for the 18RG swap.

- Car Number: 6

**Miles Smith's Runs:**

- Car Number: 16

Beat last year's time of around 54 seconds ✅ **Achieved — improved to 51.901**

**Video Status:**
As of February 3, 2025, Ian was still working on a video about the Halloweenie autocross event featuring Dale, with focus on comparing performance against the prior ~54-second run.

### First Autocross After 18RG Swap (September 22, 2024)

**Event:** KCRSCCA Event E8

**Performance:**
- Class: HCS
- Car Number: 1
- Placement: 3rd out of 4 entries
- Time: 55.762 seconds
- **This was Dale's autocross debut after the 18RG swap**

**Significance:**
This was a major milestone — the first competitive event after the massive engine swap project.

**Culmination Video Intent:**
Ian planned a video that would:
- Explain what had been done to Dale since it first got running with the new 18RG
- Show the work required to get Dale ready for autocross
- Function as the payoff to the long 6-part swap series

**Footage:**
- In-car autocross footage of Dale
- Possibly outside footage from friends

**Planned Ending:**
The ending for the autocross video was meant to communicate:
- This was only the first of many tests
- Bigger goals were ahead
- Specifically leading up to the 11th Annual Lake Garnett Grand Prix Revival
- Where Dale would face 8 track sessions

**Issues Identified:**
Although Dale survived the autocross, the following issues were identified:
- Throttle issues
- Brake bias adjustments needed
- Alignment issues

**Planned Next Steps:**
- Install roll center adjusters
- Take the car to Ryan for an alignment

The 18RG swap into Dale became a 6-part video series as of September 23, 2024.

- The car is central to Ian's content plans
