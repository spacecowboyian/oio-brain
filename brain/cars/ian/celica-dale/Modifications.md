## Modification Philosophy

Dale's modifications follow a methodical approach:

1. **Reliability First** — Address cooling, oiling, and durability before chasing power
2. **Incremental Development** — One major system at a time
3. **Test and Validate** — Each modification gets shakedown time before events
4. **Document Everything** — Every modification is content material
