Update on #dalesdragon at the Lake Garnett Grand Prix Revival

Friday night. First thing. Went to take it off the trailer and caught the exhaust coupler at the header on the dovetail of the trailer. Ripped the exhaust apart and bent the header forward and down. 

Ian went back to KC that night and got the extra stock manifold he had. Came back yesterday morning, started him up and moved Dale a bit and went to work swapping. Took all day, cobbled things together, made exhaust gaskets, bought hardware, made it work but stripped out 2 out of 4 studs that hold the intake manifold on. Once it was all back together.. he won't start. Has fuel, gas and spark. Just refuses to fire.

Ian has secured a ride for Sunday’s track event with the illustrious Mr. Al Hermans in the dubious mustang known as “Cujo”. This is not their first meeting. Ian drove Cujo at autocross in the 2021 season to a 3rd place overall (pax) finish. The car is definitely up to the challenge of the 80mph class.

As for Dale, well, there he sits. Awaiting a gaggle of kind soles to help push him back up on the trailer so he can head back to KC and begin yet another (hopefully not) year long transformation so he can be ready for next year at Garnett not to mention countless other track events.
