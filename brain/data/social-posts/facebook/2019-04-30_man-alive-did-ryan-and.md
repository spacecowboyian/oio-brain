Man alive did Ryan and Ian ever battle it out with some fierce competition yesterday at the Kansas City Region SCCA RallyCross Event #2.

Ryan placed 4th in Modified Rear Wheel drive in the K-Construction #AE86 with ONLY 3 SECONDS cumulative separating him from 1st in class. 

Ian fought a crowded field in Prepared Front Wheel drive piloting the #AXRXFX to a 3rd in class and a 5TH OVERALL!

If we would have had 2 more runs in the afternoon someone would have broken into the 39 second region for a run.. we all just kept going faster. What a fantastic day and a great site at StoneGate Motorplaza with a surface that is unique to our local programs.
