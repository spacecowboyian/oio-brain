And lo, two shims were found wanting. The stout one, 2.49, dwelt in Intake 1, and that valve was choked at .009. The lean one, 2.40, dwelt in Exhaust 3, and that valve rattled loose at .017.

And the Reverend sought new shims. But the shippers said, not in thy time. For Lake Garnett draweth nigh.

So he took up the micrometer, and it was revealed unto him: give each unto the other!

And the stout went to the loose, and the lean went to the tight. And the clearances were in spec, and it was good.
