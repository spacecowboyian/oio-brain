As the sun sets on the #rxfx and the last #kcrx event of the season we find our heroes Ryan and Ian sitting atop the ranks in their respective classes for the day. Final results aren't in yet but we're pretty sure the #corollabrotherhood stood strong today against an onslaught of higher power foes.

Ryan drove and worked all day Saturday to get the #kconstrucrion #bhrtribe #rx86 a new axle and fuel pump so it could shine today and it did not disappoint. After an early missed gate Ryan was able to claw back those 10 precious seconds on this tight and technical course and found himself right at the top of the modified rear wheel drive class after 6 runs.

Ian was faced with a doppelganger challenge as Will from the Misfits Racing Team out of topeka co-piloted the FX16 and had a lead over the prepared front wheel drive class after three runs. But cones in the afternoon negated Will's 1-2 second lead per run and allowed Ian to sneak back into the top spot.

Overall a stellar way to cap off a great season of #sccarallycross with the #kcrscca! Thanks again, and as always, to the season/event sponsor Bob Hindson Racing, Inc. for all the hard work and free soda and water! And a team OIO thanks to the #bhrtribe for providing a great place to keep our cars in tip top shape.
