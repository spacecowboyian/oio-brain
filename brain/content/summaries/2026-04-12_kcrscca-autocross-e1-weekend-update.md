## Build / Mechanical Details

### 4AG Motor (Goblin spare)
- Picked up from Henry Friday April 9
- Now on engine stand in garage
- Initial visual inspection: spark plugs likely frozen (debris in plug holes)
- Turns over easily — but main caps are loose, so minimal rotation to avoid scoring bearings
- Parts to be ordered from Battle Garage (confirmed primary source)
- Miles assigned to lead teardown, assessment, measuring, and bearing spec work
- Ian is "CEO and grunt" — buying the parts, Miles takes the technical lead

### Honda Fit Saturday Prep
- Tie rod ends inspected — all fine, nothing wrong with the steering
- Rock found and freed from front driver-side rotor by Miles
- Swapped to 15" wheels with 205 Continental 340 treadwear tires (best available for autocross)

### Fergus (BMW 2002) — Mechanical Issues Observed
- **Brakes:** Spongy pedal, almost to floor. "Hallmark of BMW 2002 brakes" per Ian. Doug has replaced some bushings but not all. Not acceptable for a race car.
- **Shifter:** No gates. Vague, must "guess" gears. Reverse and 1st nearly in the same position (both up/left on German pattern). Best method: go to 3rd → back/left to 2nd → forward to 1st.
- **Engine:** Misfires/cuts out when cam should be coming on (machine-gun stutter at rev limit). Not a rev limiter — likely spark or fuel issue. Possibly wet ignition components. Did not improve between Ian's R2 and Doug's next run.
- **Smoking:** Late in event, heavy white smoke from tailpipe on every straightaway — possibly water/coolant ingestion.

### Fitty Cent Tire Strategy (Next Event)
- **Dry event plan:** 225 tire on 7.5" wide wheel for the front, 205 340 treadwear on rear
  - Goal: grip at front, rotation at rear (Hudson and Miles both need/like rotation)
  - Budget approach: 2 wheels + 2 tires only (secondhand wheels if possible)
  - Tire compound: sticky ~200 treadwear on front
- **Wet event plan:** 340 treadwear all around; reduce rear pressure slightly below 32 psi to reduce over-rotation
- Note: Over-rotation was occurring with 32 psi square at E1 in wet
