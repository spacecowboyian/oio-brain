## Year-by-Year Highlights

### 2017
- Ian's first season: podiums at AX Events 3, 9. Class win at Event 4 (Novice/HS).
- Ryan: podium at Event 11 (CP), Halloweenie (FP).

### 2018
- Ian wins AX FSP championship and KCRX PF championship — dual season titles in one year.
- Ryan: 4th in KCRX MR season.
- Richard: 5th in KCRX PR season, class win at KSRX Armageddon (MA).
- Keegan: 4th in KCRX PA season.

### 2019
- Ryan wins dual AX championships (FSP + HCR) — best AX season in OIO history.
- Keegan wins AX EP championship.
- Ryan wins KSRX "Let It Burn" (MR class).
- Parker Jennings' first competitive outing (AX Halloweenie JB).

### 2020
- Ian wins KSRX "Bring a Friend" and "Gobbler 500" (PF class).
- Ryan: 4 event wins in AX HCR; Keegan: 2 podiums in EP.
- Ian wins MiDiv Day 1 (FSP).

### 2021
- Ian wins 3 AX season trophies (HS, FSP, CP) — most trophies in a single season.
- Ryan wins AX DP championship and KCRX MR championship.
- Ian wins KSRX November event (MF class).

### 2022
- Ian: 2 AX event wins (STX class), season trophy in STX.
- Ryan and Keegan present at multiple events.

### 2023
- Ryan wins KCRX MR championship — back-to-back seasons with the MR title (2021, 2023).
- Ian: AX DS event wins (Events 7, 8).
- Miles Smith joins the KCRX roster in the Goblin.

### 2024
- Ian: 3 KCRX MR event wins (E1, E3, E4), season trophy (2nd).
- Ryan: KCRX PF class win (E4), season trophy (2nd); KSRX wins at Dust Drags and November Endurocross.
- Richard: KSRX Dust Drags class win (SR).
- Miles: 5th in KCRX MR season (trophy-eligible field).
- Hudson Smith's debut at KCRX.

### 2025
- Ian: KCRX MR season trophy (2nd); 2 KCRX event wins (E2, E3); 2 KSRX wins (April, Plinko).
- Ryan: KCRX MF season trophy (3rd); 5+ event class wins across KCRX and KSRX.
- Miles: KCRX MR season trophy (4th) — second straight season in the trophies.
- Richard: 3 KSRX class wins (Plinko, November Endurocross, BDR).
- Hudson: active at KCRX.

### 2026
- Hudson Smith wins 2026 KCRX E1 Novice class — first class win of his career.
- Ian: 2026 KCRX E1, DNF run 7 (car failure), corrected position 7th.
- Ryan: 2026 KCRX E1, 2nd in MR.
- Miles: 2026 KCRX E1, 3rd in MR.
