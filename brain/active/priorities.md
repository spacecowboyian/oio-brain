# Active Priorities

*Season 2026 — as of March 2026*

Ranked roughly by urgency and impact. Reassess at the start of each month.

## Capacity Guardrails (Apply To All Priority Plans)

- Plan from a conservative baseline: Ian is the primary execution bottleneck.
- Keep one major execution track active at a time; defer or sequence the rest.
- If proposing parallel major tracks, name explicit owners and calendar blocks for each.
- If owners/time are unknown, mark those tracks as contingent rather than committed.

---

## 1. Get the Goblin Race-Ready

**Why it matters:** The Goblin is Ian's rallycross car and the central dramatic arc of 2026. If it's not ready, the season-long "championship run or death" story doesn't happen. It's also primary content — the rebuild needs to be documented.

**What done looks like:** Goblin starts reliably, passes a shakedown event without breaking, and Ian completes at least one full rallycross weekend in it.

**Dependencies:** Motor rebuild completion. Parts availability.

**Risks / constraints:**
- Motor rebuild complexity is unknown until fully diagnosed
- Budget — parts cost is uncertain
- Time pressure: rallycross season opener is March–April

---

## 2. Keep the Honda Fit How-To Pipeline Running

**Why it matters:** Fit How-Tos are the channel's top-of-funnel growth engine. They drive the most views by far (top video: 42K). One per month is a standing non-negotiable rule.

**What done looks like:** At least one Fit How-To published per calendar month, every month, with a queue of ideas always ready.

**Dependencies:** Kids available to wrench. Topic identified in advance.

**Risks / constraints:**
- If Ian gets busy with racing prep, this is the one that slips first — it must not
- Requires pre-planning: topic → shot list → filming day → edit

---

## 3. Document and Launch the Kids Racing Arc

**Why it matters:** Miles and Hudson racing Fitty Cent in both disciplines is a compelling, unique content arc. It ties together the access message, the kids' development, and dual-discipline tension. Mid-2026 is the target for their documented debut.

**What done looks like:** Kids' first event documented on camera, episode published, audience introduced to Miles and Hudson as competitors (not just helpers).

**Dependencies:** Event schedule confirmed. Fitty Cent in dual-discipline configuration. Kids' current restriction status (Hudson requires adult ride-along per rules).

**Risks / constraints:**
- Hudson's adult ride-along requirement affects some events
- Parker (14) still needs his permit

---

## 4. Maintain the Four-Week Content Rotation

**Why it matters:** Consistency is the channel's structural advantage. Missing a week breaks momentum and signals to the algorithm that uploads are unreliable.

**What done looks like:** No week skipped. Fit How-To in Week 1, Vlogs in Weeks 2 and 4, CoC in Week 3 (when an event has occurred). Edit budgets respected (6 hrs How-To/Vlog, 12–15 hrs CoC).

**Dependencies:** Event schedule driving CoC content. Filming happening at events and in garage.

**Risks / constraints:**
- CoC requires a racing event to recap — if events cluster, production may back up
- Solo production (Ian does everything) means illness or life events can break the schedule

---

## 5. Start Sponsorship Outreach

**Why it matters:** Racing and production costs are real and currently uncovered. The channel isn't large enough for AdSense to cover costs. Sponsorships are the path to sustainability.

**What done looks like:** At least 5 outreach messages sent to aligned potential sponsors. One active conversation in progress.

**Dependencies:** Sponsorship pitch doc is ready (it is). Outreach template is ready (it is).

**Risks / constraints:**
- At 1.93K subscribers, volume pitches are less effective — targeted, specific pitches matter more
- Must not compromise content authenticity — policy says Ian only promotes what he'd use

---

## 6. Define Dale's Pre-Lake Garnett Arc

**Why it matters:** Dale's pilgrimage to Lake Garnett in October is the season's long-form narrative. Without deliberate episode planning, it risks being undocumented or feeling shapeless.

**What done looks like:** Episode milestones for Dale are mapped out — what work happens when, which vlogs feature Dale's progress, and the Lake Garnett CoC episode is pre-planned.

**Dependencies:** Lake Garnett date confirmed. Dale's mechanical status assessed.

**Risks / constraints:**
- October is a long time away — arc risks fading if not revisited
- Dale's reliability for a high-stakes event needs to be verified

---

## 7. Launch the Website

**Why it matters:** OIO has no web presence outside YouTube. The website is the brand's front door for sponsors, new viewers, and the congregation.

**What done looks like:** Single-page site live with hero section, team, videos, and sponsorship CTA. "Take the Ride" ticket CTA functional.

**Dependencies:** Platform choice (TBD). Domain (TBD). Hero montage video cut.

**Risks / constraints:**
- Lower urgency than racing and content — but blocks sponsorship credibility
- Ian is solo — web build requires carved-out time
