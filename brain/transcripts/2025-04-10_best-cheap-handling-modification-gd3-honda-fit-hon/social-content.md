## Key Details for Engagement

**What the mod does:**
- Adds negative camber (Ryan's setup: 2.5° negative)
- Adjusts toe (Ryan chose 0.1° toe-out for better rotation)
- Makes front-wheel-drive cars more responsive and playable
- Enables lift-throttle oversteer for tighter cornering

**Why it's great:**
- Bolt-on installation (about 1 hour)
- No professional alignment required after install
- Static settings — "you get what you get"
- Affordable (~$100-150 for shims from Honed in Australia)
- Works for street, autocross, and rallycross

**Technical notes:**
- OEM bolts are 14mm, Honed includes 17mm bolts (slightly longer)
- Torque spec: 61 ft-lbs for 12mm bolts
- Tighten in a star pattern
- Optional threadlocker recommended for racing applications
- Set screws often rusted/broken on high-mileage cars

**Ryan's setup:**
- Before: 1.5° negative camber, toe in
- With plastic shims: 2.1° negative camber, zero toe
- With Honed shims: 2.5° negative camber, 0.1° toe out
- Result: Car rotates on lift-throttle, much more playable in slow-speed corners
