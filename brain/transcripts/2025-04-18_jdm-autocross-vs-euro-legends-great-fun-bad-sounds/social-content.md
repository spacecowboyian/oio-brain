## Key Details for Engagement

**The Setup:**
- Dale (1972 Toyota Celica) competing in Heritage Classic Street (HCS)
- Competition: Bill Meyer in a pristine 1974 Porsche 911S, Doug Hitchcock in a 1976 BMW 2002
- First SCCA autocross of 2025 at Metropolitan Community College driver training course, Independence, Missouri
- Ian drove Dale to the event instead of trailering (weather was perfect)

**Recent Upgrades:**
- Evil Energy overflow tank installed (no more coolant puddles on pavement)
- Techno Toy Tuning roll center adjusters on front suspension
- Roll center adjusters lowered ball joints to correct geometry after lowering ride height
- Eliminated bump steer, made car more predictable, stable, and composed

**The Race:**
- Dale briefly took the class lead with a 50.986 time, beating Doug's BMW 2002
- Doug and Bill both eventually scored lower 50s times
- On third run, Ian heard loud rattling noise from motor ("coffee can full of bolts")
- Decided to stop racing and trailer the car home before the fourth heat
- Miles (co-driver) had also done three runs, so motor had six runs total with the noise

**The Outcome:**
- Called it a "semi-successful shakedown"
- Car was fast and handled well with the new suspension
- Got close to beating the Porsche and BMW
- Mechanical issue cut the day short (future diagnosis video coming)

**Quote:**
"As I rolled across the finish and I let the motor wind down, I heard a familiar but louder noise... it sounded like a coffee can full of bolts just bouncing around in the motor."
