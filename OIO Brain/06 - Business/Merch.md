# Merch

> OIO merchandise — ideas, status, and logistics.

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## Status

Merch is a planned future revenue stream. Not yet launched as of March 2026.

---

## Merch Philosophy

OIO merch should:
- Feel like something a congregation member actually wants to wear
- Reference the bits without requiring explanation
- Be simple — over-designed merch doesn't sell
- Reflect the brand palette: black, white, yellow, red, orange

**It should not:**
- Look like a car show vendor booth
- Be logo-slap merch with no personality

## Production Options

| Platform | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Printful / Printify (POD) | No inventory risk, easy to start | Lower margins |
| Fourthwall | Creator-focused, integrates with YouTube | |
| Spring (Teespring) | YouTube integration | Commoditized feel |
| Local print run | Higher quality, better margins | Upfront cost, inventory risk |
