# Website

> OIO website plan, direction, and status.

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## Status

Planned. Not yet launched as of March 2026.

---

## Vision

A simple one-page site introducing the brand, featuring the team, highlighting recent videos, and presenting sponsorship opportunities.

---

## Design Direction

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Background | Black |
| Headings / CTAs | Helvetica Neue style |
| Body font | IBM Plex Sans |
| Palette | High-contrast: black, white, yellow, red, orange |
| Feel | Strong, fast, not corporate |

---

## Page Structure

### Hero Section
- Fast-cut video montage background
- OIO brand statement
- **"Take the Ride" CTA** — styled as a vintage orange carnival ticket
  - Rips on click
  - Shows current subscriber count + 1 (for the viewer)

### About / Brand Section
- What OIO is (one-line description)
- The Church of Combustion bit, briefly
- The mission: making motorsports accessible

### Team Section
- Ian + key congregation members
- Brief bios, car photos
- Personality — not resume format

### Videos Section
- Recent / top-performing videos embedded or linked
- Separate Honda Fit How-To call-out (the content that gets people in)

### Sponsorship Section
- Clean pitch for potential sponsors
- Link to [Sponsorship Pitch](../01%20-%20Brand/Sponsorship-Pitch.md) or contact form

### Footer
- Social links
- Channel link
- Contact
