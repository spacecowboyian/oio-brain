# Titles and Thumbnails

> OIO's approach to titles and thumbnails. Patterns that work, patterns to avoid, and a working list of title drafts.

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## Title Principles

**OIO titles are practical and searchable for How-Tos. They can be punchy and character-driven for vlogs and Church of Combustion.**

### Honda Fit How-To Titles
- Lead with the search term
- Keep it specific (model year/gen if relevant)
- Secondary hook after the dash or in parens is fine
- Avoid clickbait — the audience finds these through search, not recommendation

**Pattern:** `[Task] — [Car/Gen] | [Optional hook]`

**Examples:**
- "Honda Fit Armrest Hack — GD3 (Easy Fix)"
- "Honda Fit Valve Adjustment — GE8 Step by Step"
- "Honda Fit Battery Replacement — 5 Minutes, No Dealer"

### Vlog Titles
- Should hint at what's at stake or what changes this episode
- Tie to the active arc (Goblin, Dale, etc.)
- Don't overstate the drama but don't hide it either

**Pattern:** `[What happened / what's at stake] | [Car/Arc name]`

**Examples:**
- "The Goblin Has a Pulse Again"
- "Dale Gets New Shoes for Lake Garnett"
- "We Put the Kids in the Car and See What Happens"

### Church of Combustion Titles
- Can be more theatrical
- Reference the event and the bit
- The congregation knows the format — reward them

**Examples:**
- "The Church of Combustion — Lake Garnett Grand Prix Revival 2025"
- "Sunday Service: The Goblin Returns to the Dirt"

---

## Thumbnail Principles

- **High contrast.** The palette is black, white, yellow, red, orange.
- **One clear focal point.** Either the car, Ian's face, or a key prop.
- **Text is large and legible at small size.** Test at 150px wide.
- **Don't obscure the car.** People are here for the cars.
- **Church of Combustion thumbnails** can use the sepia aesthetic.
- **How-To thumbnails** should show the task clearly — not just a face.
