# Shorts and Social Posts

> OIO's approach to short-form content and social media. What to post, when, and how.

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## Philosophy

Shorts and social posts are:
1. **Top-of-funnel** — they can bring in new viewers who don't find long-form first
2. **Congregation maintenance** — keep the audience warm between uploads
3. **Clip repurposing** — extract value from footage that already exists

OIO is a one-person operation. Social should be efficient, not exhausting.

---

## Shorts Strategy

**Best source material for Shorts:**
- Goblin suffering moments — mechanical failure in real time
- Kids wrenching / surprising moments in How-Tos
- Event moments — close calls, dust clouds, cone penalties
- Project Board reveals — especially when it's overwhelming
- Any moment where Ian is definitively wrong about something

**Format:**
- Hook in first 2 seconds — don't ease in
- 30–60 seconds ideal
- Captions on (auto-gen is fine, correct errors)
- End with a question or tease of the full video

**When to cut a Short:**
- Any time a clip makes you laugh on its own
- Any time a How-To has a satisfying standalone moment
- Any event run that tells a complete story in under 60 seconds

## Social Post Strategy

### YouTube Community Tab
- Use for: behind-the-scenes peeks, Project Board updates, event day announcements, congregation polls
- Cadence: 1–2 per week
- Voice: Same as vlog — casual, honest, Ian talking to friends

### Instagram / TikTok (if applicable)
- Repurpose Shorts content
- Car photos from events and garage
- Behind-the-scenes build progress
- Use relevant hashtags: #autocross #rallycross #hondafit #projectcar #grassrootsmotorsports

## Post Templates

### Pre-Upload Hype Post
```
New video [drops/is up] Thursday.

[One-line tease of what's at stake or what goes wrong]

[Link / notification reminder]
```

### Event Day Post
```
Race day. [Location]. [Car(s) running].

[One honest line about expectations — low or high]

Full recap [coming Sunday / next week].
```

### Project Board Update
```
Updated the Project Board.

[Cards added]. [Cards removed]. [One card that's been there too long].

The Goblin card is still there. Obviously.
```

### Congregation Ask / Poll
```
Quick question for the congregation:

[Question relevant to upcoming content or decision]

[Option A / Option B]
```
