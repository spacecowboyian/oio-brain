## What This Video Is About

Ian and Miles test-drive the Honda Fit (Fitty Cent) after replacing the slave cylinder to address a clutch hydraulic failure. The clutch is no longer sticking, but engagement is extremely low — almost at the floor — suggesting air is still trapped somewhere in the system, likely in the master cylinder. The drive covers a few blocks and roundabouts while Ian and Miles diagnose the remaining issue on camera.

---

## Key Topics Covered

- **Slave cylinder replaced** — confirmed leaking, replacement done
- **Clutch engagement very low** — pedal engages only at the last inch from the floor
- **Air in hydraulics** — Ian suspects air in the master cylinder; gravity bleed and foot bleed didn't fully purge the system
- **Master cylinder suspect** — Ian mentions the master is a cheap Chinese unit, possibly the wrong spec, installed years ago during clutch work
- **Bench bleed may be required** — Ian doesn't want to do this because the master is under the cowl (difficult access)
- **Pressure bleed alternative** — Plan to go to Ryan's shop and use a pressure bleeder
- **Continental DWS O6 tires** — praised as excellent 500 treadwear tire; Ian notes they no longer make 15" sizes
- **DOT 3 fluid** — mentioned; someone had accidentally topped off the clutch reservoir from the brake reservoir (mixed DOT 3 into DOT 4 brakes), but brakes feel fine
- **Car is driveable** — clutch comes back up, car moves under its own power — "success enough"

## Build / Mechanical Details

- **Slave cylinder:** Replaced (was leaking out the front, confirmed bad)
- **Clutch mileage:** ~60,000 miles since last clutch replacement — worn but not dead. Ian and kids are hard on clutches.
- **Clutch adjustment:** Ian had adjusted the pedal while the hydraulics were bad; may need to re-adjust now that slave is replaced
- **Fluid:** DOT 3 in clutch system; someone previously added DOT 3 to the brake reservoir as well (inadvertent mix into DOT 4)
- **Master cylinder:** Cheap Chinese unit, possibly wrong spec — installed with original clutch work
- **Tires:** Continental DWS O6, 500 treadwear — Ian confirms they are very good but no longer available in 15"
- **Pedal feel:** Engages at ~1 inch from floor; 3–4 inches of dead travel with no resistance before engagement
- **Root cause hypothesis:** Air in master cylinder; bench bleed or pressure bleed needed
- **Planned next step:** Pressure bleed at Ryan's shop, or replace master cylinder
