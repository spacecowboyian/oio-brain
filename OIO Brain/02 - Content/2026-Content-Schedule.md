## Algorithm Notes

- **Shorts drive top-of-funnel.** The 5 highest-performing 2026 videos are all shorts or Fit content. Every event and every garage session should produce at least one short.
- **Fit How-Tos are non-negotiable.** One per month, every month. This is the channel's growth engine. Missing February and March hurt — do not miss again.
- **Consistency > perfection.** A shorter vlog published on time beats a longer one published 2 weeks late. Hit every Thursday.
- **COC is the prestige format.** Don't rush them. If a race happens and you don't have compelling footage, drop a Sunday School instead of a weak COC.

## Shorts Strategy

Shorts are bonus content that run alongside the regular schedule. They are not in the rotation, but they are critical for top-of-funnel growth.

**When to capture shorts:**
- Every race event: 30-second clips of crashes, great runs, funny moments, car sounds
- Every garage session: weird tools, satisfying processes, before/after moments
- Anything the kids do: always performs (see: armrest hack, GE8 mud run)
- Equipment oddities: strange tools, funny failures

**Historical shorts that performed well:**
- Rubber bushing removal (FX16) — 85K views
- Maxpeedingrods coilover setup — 60K views
- GE8 front sway bar replacement — 14K views
- Fit breaks stuff (power test) — 17K views

**Target:** At least 2–3 shorts per month, published between long-form videos.
