# Policies

> OIO's standing operating policies. These define how the channel and brand operate consistently.

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## Content Policies

### Standing Rules (Never Break)
1. **One Honda Fit How-To per month, every month, forever.**
2. **Rallycross and autocross are NOT the same.** Never flatten them together in copy, titles, or descriptions.
3. **Church of Combustion episodes drop on Sunday.** No exceptions.
4. **Ian is always the butt of the joke.** The cars humble him. He documents it on purpose.
5. **6-hour edit cap** on How-Tos and Vlogs. Ship it.
6. **12–15 hours** on Church of Combustion only.
7. **Church of Combustion drops 1–2 weeks post-event.** Never same-day.

### Content Quality
- Every video must deliver on its title — no bait-and-switch
- How-To videos must leave the viewer able to do the task
- Never condescend to the audience. They are smart.
- If it's not interesting yet, find the angle — don't skip it

### Kids on Camera
- Miles, Hudson, and Parker appear with consent
- Kids' roles: wrenching on How-Tos (with Ian supervising), racing documented events
- No content that puts kids in a bad light or exploits their mistakes

## Brand Policies

### Voice Consistency
- Always write/speak in OIO voice — see [Voice and Tone](../01%20-%20Brand/Voice-and-Tone.md)
- No corporate language in any OIO communication
- Ian is never portrayed as polished or aspirational — he's the everyman

### Music and Assets
- All music must be licensed — see [Music and Assets](../05%20-%20Production/Music-and-Assets.md)
- No unauthorized use of third-party footage or music
- Credit required for any Creative Commons content

---

## Business Policies

### Sponsorships
- No sponsorship deal that compromises content authenticity
- Sponsored content must be disclosed per FTC guidelines (paid partnership label, verbal mention)
- Ian only promotes products he would actually use or has used
- All sponsorship terms documented in writing before content is made

### Financial
- All OIO expenses tracked in [Expenses](../06%20-%20Business/Expenses.md)
- Revenue tracked separately from personal finances
- Sponsorship payments invoiced and documented

---

## Data and Security Policies

- Passwords stored in password manager only — never in documents
- Account credentials documented by handle/username only — see [Accounts](Accounts.md)
- Raw footage backed up to minimum two locations before editing
- Published video files archived after upload
