## GE8 vs GD3 Notes

OIO's Fit is the **GE8** (2009). Ryan's Fit is a **GD3** (2007). They are different cars for content purposes:
- GE8: 5-speed manual (or auto), revised suspension
- GD3: Some variants had paddle-shift auto, different suspension
- Specify the generation in all How-To titles and descriptions
