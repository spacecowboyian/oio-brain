## Rallycross-Specific Notes

The MR2 AW11 is a mid-engine, rear-wheel-drive car. Rallycross setup priorities:

- **Durability over precision** — the car needs to survive the surface, not just be fast
- **Suspension travel** — more bump clearance than an autocross setup
- **Tires** — aggressive, puncture-resistant
- **Skid plates / underprotection** — mid-engine means vulnerable sump
