## Background

Keegan's Honda Prelude is a first-generation car — likely an '82 or '83 — and it is one of the wilder builds in the congregation. The car sits on airbags and is lowered. It currently runs its stock motor. The bodywork has been completely transformed: wide flares and a custom body kit fabricated in metal by a local Kansas City fabricator give it an aggressive, one-of-a-kind look.

It's currently in an unpainted state — raw fabrication work visible, similar to the MGB GT at its bare-metal stage. It is not perfect, but it is very cool.

Not yet featured on the OIO channel as of March 2026.
