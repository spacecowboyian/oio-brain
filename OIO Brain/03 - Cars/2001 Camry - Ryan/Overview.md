## Background

Ryan's 2001 Toyota Camry is a lifted, big-tired chaos machine being built for the Gambler 500. The OIO description: *"if 7th graders made their own Mad Max movie in their backyard."* That is exactly right.

The 2001 Car debuted as an OIO subject in July 2025, with a trailer, teaser, and short-form content building up the reveal. It appeared again in Ian's January 2026 shop update video.

The car is the opposite of motorsports precision. It is grassroots adventure content at its most unhinged.
