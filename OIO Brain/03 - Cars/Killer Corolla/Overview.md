## Background

A 1977 Toyota Corolla Sport Coupe loaded with vintage TRD parts. Dropped a valve mid-autocross approximately five years ago. Has sat since. The current plan involves a $100 mystery motor and a very long list of TRD components.

The resurrection arc is one of the longest-running stories in OIO history. The car has been down for six years. 2026 is when it starts coming back.
