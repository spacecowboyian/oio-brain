# Rallycross

---

## What Is Rallycross

Rallycross is a timed motorsport discipline run on mixed surfaces — typically dirt, gravel, grass, or a combination. Cars run solo against the clock, similar to autocross, but on unpaved courses.

**Key characteristics:**
- Surface: Dirt, gravel, grass, or mixed
- Course: Typically larger than autocross, more flowing
- Timer: Solo runs against the clock
- Organization: SCCA RallyCross, regional clubs, local events

---

## Rallycross ≠ Autocross

**This matters. Never flatten them together.**

| | Rallycross | Autocross |
|---|---|---|
| Surface | Dirt/gravel/mixed | Paved parking lot |
| Tire needs | Aggressive, puncture-resistant | Soft, high-grip |
| Setup priorities | Durability, suspension travel | Precision, low roll |
| Driving demands | Momentum, commitment, loose-surface feel | Accuracy, late apex |
| Prep decisions | Protect the car | Optimize the car |
| Cone penalty | Sometimes cones, sometimes flags | Cones everywhere |

## Car Prep for Rallycross

### Pre-Event Checklist
- [ ] Tires — correct spec (aggressive tread, proper inflation for dirt)
- [ ] Skid plate / underprotection in place
- [ ] Fluids topped — oil, coolant, brake fluid
- [ ] Brake inspection — pads and rotors
- [ ] Air filter — don't run with a clogged filter on a dusty course
- [ ] Safety equipment — helmet, harness if required
- [ ] Numbers/registration materials in car

### Post-Event Checklist
- [ ] Undercar inspection — check for damage from rocks/debris
- [ ] Tire condition — cuts, punctures, wear
- [ ] Check for unusual noises before driving home
- [ ] Brake inspection if heavy use
- [ ] Air filter condition
