# KCRSCCA RallyCross — OIO Driver Results 2017–2024

## Data Source and Limitations

- **Source:** KCRSCCA RallyCross results archive, 2017–2024
- **Tracked drivers:** Ian Jennings, Miles Smith, Ryan Redenbaugh, Hudson Smith
- **Known gaps:** 2021 and 2022 archive pages returned HTTP 429 rate-limit errors during collection; no tracked-driver appearances confirmed for those years
- **Hudson Smith:** No appearances found in the 2017–2024 archive. He competes in 2025 (outside this range).
- Season-level championship standings are not fully captured here. See each driver section for trophy notes.
