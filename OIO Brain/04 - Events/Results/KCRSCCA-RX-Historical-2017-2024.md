# KCRSCCA Competition History — OIO Drivers 2017–2024

> Consolidated event-level results for all OIO tracked drivers in KCRSCCA Autocross and RallyCross competition.

*Last updated: March 2026 — processed from KCRSCCA results archive and scraped event data*

## Data Source and Coverage

- **Source:** KCRSCCA results archive (2017–2024) + supplementary scraped data (kcrscca.org)
- **Tracked drivers:** Ian Jennings, Miles Smith, Ryan Redenbaugh, Keegan Wilhelm
- **Coverage:**
  - AX 2021: Events 1–3 confirmed
  - AX 2022: Events 1–6 confirmed (7–10 blocked by proxy)
  - AX 2023: Events 1–4 confirmed
  - AX 2024: All 10 events confirmed
  - RX 2022: Event 3 confirmed
  - RX 2023: Events 1 and 3 confirmed
  - RX 2024: All 7 events confirmed
  - RX 2017–2020: Ian and Ryan results from original zip archive
- **Richard Thompson:** No KCRSCCA results found. Competes via KSRX (inaccessible).
- **Hudson Smith:** No appearances in 2017–2024. Competes starting 2025 (outside this range).
