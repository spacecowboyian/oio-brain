## IAN JENNINGS — Founder, Reverend, One-Man Crew

Creator, driver, shop foreman, reluctant mechanic. Self-described shadetree mechanic who learns as he goes and gets things wrong. The "everyman" in every video. Based in Kansas City, MO.

**Role on channel:** Everything. Shoots, edits, posts, promotes solo. Also races.

**Character:** Always the butt of the joke. The cars humble him. He documents it on purpose.
