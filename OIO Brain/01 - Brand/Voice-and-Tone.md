# Voice and Tone

> How OIO writes, speaks, and sounds. Use this before writing any script, post, title, description, or caption.

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## The Short Version

**Lonely Island meets Vice Grip Garage.**

Absurdist comedy commitment meets folksy Midwest "will it run" drama. Ian is always the butt of the joke. The cars humble him. He documents it on purpose.

---

## Core Voice Qualities

| Quality | What It Means | What It Sounds Like |
|---|---|---|
| **Practical** | Real constraints, real solutions. No fantasy mod lists. | "This costs $12 and takes 45 minutes" not "you could also consider a turbo upgrade" |
| **Enthusiastic** | Genuine love for the cars and the sport | The energy is real. Not performed hype. |
| **Hands-on** | Show the work. Dirty fingernails. | Narrate while doing the thing, not after |
| **Mildly irreverent** | Knowledgeable garage talk. Never corporate. | "She makes a noise like a bag of loose change" |
| **Encouraging** | You can do this too. Seriously. | Acknowledge the fear, then show it's fine |
| **Self-deprecating** | Ian gets it wrong. A lot. | "That's not right. That's incredibly not right." |

## Tone Shifts by Format

### Honda Fit How-To
- **Tone:** Practical, direct, clear
- **Energy:** Calm confidence. The kids know what they're doing.
- **Language:** Accessible. Define terms once, briefly. Don't talk down.
- **Goal:** Leave the viewer knowing exactly how to do the thing

### Vlog
- **Tone:** Casual, behind-the-scenes, honest
- **Energy:** Mid. Not overproduced energy. Real garage talk.
- **Language:** Conversational. Like Ian's talking to a friend in the shop.
- **Goal:** Move arcs forward, show the project board, make the viewer feel in the room

### Church of Combustion
- **Tone:** Committed bit. Reverent absurdism.
- **Energy:** Elevated. This is the big one.
- **Language:** The Reverend voice — slightly formal, slightly theatrical, dead serious about the joke
- **Goal:** Make the racing event feel like a religious experience, including the mechanical suffering

### Social / Shorts
- **Tone:** Quick, punchy, direct
- **Energy:** High
- **Language:** Short sentences. One idea per post.
- **Goal:** Hook fast, deliver the thing, get the click

## Things to Never Do

- **Never be corporate.** No brand-voice copy. No "We're passionate about delivering..."
- **Never be condescending.** The audience is smart. Don't over-explain or hedge everything.
- **Never flatten rallycross and autocross.** They are different. Treat them differently.
- **Never make Ian the hero.** He's the one who gets humbled. The cars are the heroes.
- **Never punch up or punch down.** The humor is self-directed.
- **Never fake enthusiasm.** If it's not interesting, find the angle that makes it interesting.

## Signature Phrases and Patterns

- Sign-off: *"Until next time, remember to go out and find your own apex, because it's better late than better late than never."*
- *"The congregation..."* — referring to the audience
- *"Sportsmanlike Driving says..."* — referencing the sacred text
- The Project Board is opened and closed every vlog. Always.
- The Goblin is never fine. Never assume it's fine.

---

## Writing Checklist

Before publishing any written content:

- [ ] Does it sound like Ian, or does it sound like a brand manager?
- [ ] Is Ian the butt of the joke (if there is a joke)?
- [ ] Does it treat the audience as intelligent?
- [ ] Is it honest about cost and effort?
- [ ] Does it distinguish rallycross and autocross if both are mentioned?
- [ ] Is it free of corporate or influencer language?
