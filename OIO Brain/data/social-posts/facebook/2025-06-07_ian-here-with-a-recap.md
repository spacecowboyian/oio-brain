Ian here with a recap of a big week of work on my boy Dale, the 72 Celica

What started as a mystery knock turned into a full-blown teardown of Dale’s 18RG. I thought it was a bad timing chain slipper. It wasn’t. But by the time I figured that out, the engine was already in pieces.

The car sat on jack stands for weeks. Bolts and tools were scattered. Parts were in piles. I spent hours on my back underneath it. Then somehow, in just a few days, it all went back together.

I adjusted the cam timing for high-RPM power. Replaced all the rod bearings after finding copper in the pan. Swapped in the lightened flywheel, a fresh clutch, and the original transmission. I even had to lift the engine just to get the oil pan back on. I lost the imported water pump gasket and had to hand-cut a new one… it’s exhausing just thinking about it all again.

Then Friday night, just as I was about to reach the goal of getting the car to Miles’ school car show on Saturday morning, I fired it up again. It ran. It smoked. I fixed the catch can. I started tuning. I was almost there.

About 10 starts in.. out of nowhere… the starter gave up… and so did I. Dale won’t make it to the show.

But he’ll be hitting the streets again very soon, thanks to the effort Miles and I put in this week. I couldn’t have even gotten close without him.
