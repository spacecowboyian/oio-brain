Ryan here,
So you all might be wondering what's up with the Corvair. Years before I owned a Toyota race car I co drove a Corvair with my good buddy Aaron. I drove it 4 times that year and was hooked on autocross. I've done track events before but never autocross. It was intoxicating. And the Corvair was my first love. I drove the 86 daily, and while racing the Corvair I turned the 86 into a autocross car.

The 86 was terrible at autocross because it didn't have enough power. Hence why it became a Rallycross car. Well life happened and Aaron got busy and the Corvair sat. 

A couple of weeks ago it still sounded like life was gonna be too busy for Aaron to run it this year as well. But low and behold I got a text on Friday asking me if I want to co-drive. Well yeah of course!! 

So the plan originally was to make my new to me 85 Celica GTS a autocross car but given recent developments I'm gonna run the Corvair. So if you all are interested I'll post more stories of the "red car" cause we're going to Nationals in it this year. It's out of our wheel house but a completely unique, getting rarer every day car.
