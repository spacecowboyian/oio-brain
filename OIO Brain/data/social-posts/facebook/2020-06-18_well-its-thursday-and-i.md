Well it's Thursday and I think we've finally gotten over the high from last Sunday's Kansas City Region - Sports Car Club of America Rallycross. Ryan did his best Takumi impression and slid in for 4th in modified rear wheel drive in the #kconstruction #bhrtribe #ae86. I-35 speedway events are challenging because of the mix of the hardpack oval and the soft sandy center so the trick is to find the right tire and hope it grips all the way around.
