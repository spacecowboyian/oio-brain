Dale update: Road to Lake Garnett Grand Prix Revival.

Clutch was finally repaired and I decided to replace the oil pan gasket (which did not leak) This took a lot longer than it should and involved removing the steering system which I also decided to replace with a rebuilt box, new pitman arm and all new tie rod ends. Today the front end was aligned and I replaced a stuck driver's rear wheel cylinder, removed a "saftey braker" from the system and flushed it with Dot 4. Also replaced the front pads with something a bit more stock feeling. The brakes work now but not as well as I would like. Pads still need bedding. So the stopping safely part is solved. Now we must focus on going faster.

The engine hasn't revved freely since I replaced the weber 32/36 that was dumping fuel down the hatch. I bought a carb jetted for a 20R motor figuring that would be plenty for a cammed 18R.. it is not. So tomorrow I put in a larger main jet and hope that even if it runs rich.. it will still rev out and get me some speed. Otherwise Dale may not make an appearance at Lake Garnett this year.

Cross all your fingers for me,

Ian
