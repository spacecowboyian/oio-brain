## Background

Keegan's first-generation 1982 Honda Prelude is ice blue with a custom engine build: an EL1 non-CVCC head installed on the EK1 block, fed by a Weber 32/36 carburetor. The suspension has been converted to coilover-style air bags on all four corners (replacing the springs). Wears 280TW tires and a DIY body kit.
