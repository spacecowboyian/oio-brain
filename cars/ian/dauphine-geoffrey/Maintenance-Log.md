## Log

### 2026-04-05 — Initial Assessment / Seized Plug Attempt

**Summary:** Ian and Parker opened Geoffrey to see whether the engine could be freed up. The car cover had blown off and was plastered against the boat next to it. The spark plugs were rusted in place, so the planned Marvel Mystery Oil soak through the plug holes did not happen. Ian confirmed the hand-crank setup exists and routes through the bumper/body, but the engine would not budge by hand crank.

**Current status:**
- Car is not running
- Engine is locked solid by hand
- Spark plugs are rusted in place
- Cover needs to be secured properly before the next attempt

**Follow-up (same day or next day):** Ian returned with Free All penetrant, sprayed it on the spark plugs and surrounding area, and walked away. Left it to soak for approximately 2 days before the next visit.

**Planned next steps:**
- ✅ Applied Free All — soaking for 2 days
- Retry spark plug extraction after soak
- If plugs still will not move, use an induction heater/tool
- Buy a personal induction tool instead of borrowing Ryan's again if the seized-plug approach becomes recurring

**Open questions:**
- Whether the plugs can come out without thread damage
- Whether penetrant alone will free the plugs or whether heat will be required
- Whether the engine is seized from corrosion, internal damage, or both

---

### 2026-04-07 — Free All / Plugs Out / Marvel Mystery Oil Soak

**Summary:** Third work session. Free All had been soaking for approximately 2 days. Ian applied another round of Free All and, with some coaxing, removed all four spark plugs. The distributor also had to be pulled to get access. Using a 1960s-era metal funnel (the only one on hand), Ian poured Marvel Mystery Oil down each spark plug hole — approximately 895 ccs total, though some spilled on the ground during the process. A full reserve bottle of MMO is on hand if more is needed. Valve cover removed — interior found to be very rusty. MMO also poured into the valve cover to let it seep past the seals. Engine is still locked; everything left to soak.

**Stated goal:** Get the Renault motor running just to say it was done. Make Geoffrey move under its own power for the first time in approximately 40 years. After that, likely park it and replace what's underneath.

**Current status:**
- All spark plugs removed ✅
- Distributor removed ✅
- ~895 ccs Marvel Mystery Oil added through spark plug holes
- Valve cover removed — interior is very rusty
- Marvel Mystery Oil poured into valve cover to seat past seals
- Engine rotation still unknown — not yet attempted with plugs out
- Full reserve bottle of MMO available if more is needed

**Planned next steps:**
- Attempt to turn engine by hand crank (with plugs now out)
- If hand crank won't free it: put car in gear and push it back and forth to use transmission to help break the engine loose
- Document whether the motor frees up

**Open questions:**
- Will the engine free up with the plugs out and MMO soaking?
- Will the transmission push method work if hand crank fails?
- How much internal rust/damage is there given the rusty valve cover interior?
