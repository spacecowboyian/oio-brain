# Dale — Build History

> Comprehensive history of the 18RG engine swap and post-swap development phases

*Last updated: April 2026*

---

## Overview

Dale's recent history centers around a major 18RG engine swap that became a 6-part video series, followed by extensive sorting, tuning, and reliability work. The build expanded beyond the minimum viable "get the car running and driving" scope into a more comprehensive development project.

---

## Major Build Milestone: 18RG Engine Swap

### The Six-Part Series

Ian's work swapping the 18RG into Dale became a 6-part video series, indicating:
- The swap was substantial enough to warrant long-form documented build arc
- The car is central to Ian's content plans
- The project involved enough complexity, setbacks, and progress to produce multiple episodes
- The culmination video was intended to close one chapter and transition Dale into active use/testing

### State at End of Swap Phase

At the conclusion of the initial swap work:
- Dale had just gotten running with the new 18RG engine
- This was the **start** of the sorting phase, not the finish line
- The line between "swap complete" and "car actually ready" was large

---

## Biggest Challenges Identified During the Build

### 1. Cooling System Integrity

**The Problem:**
- Getting the system to hold coolant
- Due to cracks and leaks in the thermostat housing
- One of the clearest recurring mechanical problems associated with Dale

**Why It Matters:**
This was not a cosmetic issue. It directly affects:
- Reliability
- Confidence in testing
- Event readiness
- Risk of overheating
- Quality of content (cooling failures can ruin the story arc of an event video)

**Impact:**
Ian wanted to highlight in a later video how insufficient work on the coolant system ultimately cost him the race. This was not just a garage annoyance — it had real-world competitive consequences.

### 2. Dash and Switch Work

**The Problem:**
- Installing the dash
- Swapping/moving switches

**Project Creep Issue:**
Ian specifically noted this was:
- Not necessary for the engine swap
- Probably not the best project to take on at the same time

This is a classic project-creep issue: the build expanded beyond minimum scope, which added complexity and slowed progress.

### 3. Exhaust Work

**The Problem:**
Ian explicitly identified "getting the exhaust right" as one of the biggest challenges.

This suggests the exhaust was either:
- Hard to package
- Hard to route
- Hard to make fit well
- Hard to make work acceptably with the new engine setup

## Post-Swap Sorting Work

Once Dale was running, Ian started the less glamorous but critical work that turns a swapped car into a usable one.

### Work Done to Get Dale Ready for First Autocross

Ian listed the following tasks:
- ✅ Put around 200 miles on the car (shakedown phase)
- ✅ Tightened the throttle cable
- ✅ Installed a new rubber brake pedal cover
- ⚠️ Tried, but failed, to fix the non-blinking turn signals
- ✅ Changed the oil
- ✅ Changed the transmission gear oil
- ✅ Checked the spark plugs
- ✅ Loaded the car onto the trailer

**What This Says:**
This was a genuine shakedown phase, not a polished final state. The 200-mile run-in is especially important — it suggests Ian was trying to:
- Build confidence in the car
- Expose weak points before competition
- Verify fluids, drivability, and basic reliability
- Identify issues that only show up under real use

## First Competitive Return After the Swap

### First Autocross Since the Engine Swap

Ian specifically framed one video around:
- Explaining the work done to Dale since the last video
- Showing the work involved in getting Dale ready for its first autocross since the engine swap

**Why It Matters:**
For a project car like Dale, the first event after a big swap is one of the most revealing moments because it tests:
- Cooling
- Throttle response
- Braking
- Alignment
- Reliability under repeated load
- Driver confidence
- Whether the swap is actually integrated or merely functional

---

## Issues Identified After Autocross Testing

Ian noted that although Dale survived the autocross, the following issues were identified:
- **Throttle issues** — Control/drivability problems
- **Brake bias adjustments needed** — Braking system not fully balanced for performance driving
- **Alignment issues** — Front-end geometry not yet optimized

This is a highly valuable snapshot because it shows the car was functional enough to compete, but still clearly in development.

### Planned Next Steps After That Event

Ian planned to:
- Install roll center adjusters
- Take the car to Ryan for an alignment

This indicates the chassis setup was still behind the engine progress and needed more serious geometry correction.

## 18RG-Related Parts & Planning

These items have been discussed in the Dale/18RG context and are relevant to the engine's evolving state:

**Timing System:**
- Upper timing chain
- Lower timing chain
- Secondary idler gear replacing the factory middle slipper in the upper timing system
- Timing chain tick concern (possible slack)

**Engine Internals:**
- Full engine overhaul gasket kit
- OEM-thickness head gasket for standard bore
- Standard bearings
- Standard bore / gasket thickness discussion

**Cooling:**
- Thermostat housing issues (major recurring problem)
- Possible thermostat replacement
- Cooling system sealing issues

**Ignition & Fuel:**
- Distributor timing adjustment
- Carb accelerator pump adjustment
- Jetting as a longer-term tuning area

**Maintenance:**
- Oil changes after tuning/testing
- Long-term oiling and cooling improvements planned
