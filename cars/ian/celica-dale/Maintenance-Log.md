## Cooling System History

Cooling is one of the most repeated themes in Dale's history and has had real competitive consequences.

### Thermostat Housing — Major Recurring Problem

**The Core Issue:**
- Cracks and leaks in the thermostat housing
- Causing problems getting the cooling system to hold coolant
- One of the clearest recurring mechanical problems associated with Dale

**Impact on Reliability:**
This was not a cosmetic issue. It directly affected:
- Reliability
- Confidence in testing
- Event readiness
- Risk of overheating
- Quality of content (cooling failures can ruin the story arc of an event video)

**Competitive Consequences:**
Ian wanted to highlight in a later video how insufficient work on the coolant system **ultimately cost him the race**. This was not just a garage annoyance — it had real-world competitive impact.

### Thermostat Concerns

**Status:** Suspected issue

At one point Ian planned to:
- Inspect whether the thermostat was stuck open
- Replace it if necessary

This was connected to broader tuning and prep work.

### Current Status

**Known Issues:**
- Final cooling-system robustness still a concern
- Thermostat status/behavior needs verification

**Future Plans:**
- Improved cooling system as part of higher-compression 18RG build
- More robust cooling solution to support track use

### Oil Changes History

#### Pre-First Autocross (September 2024)

- ✅ Engine oil

As part of event prep after the 18RG swap.

#### Post-Timing Work (October 2024)

Ian planned to:
1. Measure timing
2. Possibly replace thermostat
3. Tune the car
4. Change the oil and filter again before autocross

**Interpretation:**
This suggests Ian is cautious about contamination / break-in / repeated testing on a fresh or recently altered setup.

### Oil Change Philosophy

Ian's approach has been:
- Frequent oil changes during initial shakedown period
- Oil changes after significant tuning sessions
- Monitoring for contamination or issues during break-in
- Treating each major change (timing, tuning, etc.) as a reason to refresh fluids
