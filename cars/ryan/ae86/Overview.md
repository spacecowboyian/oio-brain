## Background

Ryan's AE86 is one of the original congregation cars and one of the longest-running subjects on the OIO channel. Ryan has been racing and wrenching on this car since at least 2019. It's been a rallycross competitor for years — bumper weights removed, popup headlights pulled, shifter bushing swapped, and countless events logged.

As of 2026 the car is down, waiting on a planned 1UZ V8 swap. The goal is to build it into a national-level rallycross contender. This is the biggest build arc Ryan has had on the channel.

The AE86 has 16 published OIO videos, going back to early 2019.
