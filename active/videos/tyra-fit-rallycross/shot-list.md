# Tyra Fit Video - Detailed Shot List

## Interview (Tyra)

**Setup:**
- Location: Pit area
- Camera: Tripod, 5 ft from Tyra
- Background: Car behind/beside (context)
- Audio: Lavalier mic on shirt
- Lighting: Natural light, face well-lit
- Test: 10 sec before actual interview

**Questions:**
1. What happened to your Fit?
2. How did the community help rebuild it?
3. How does it feel to drive it for the first time in a race?
4. What's next for you and the car?

**Technique:** Hit record, ask, shut up and let her finish. Take notes on good quotes.

---

## B-Roll Shots

### Car Exterior
- Overhead/drone if available
- Driver side full (30 sec)
- Passenger side full (30 sec)
- Rear (30 sec)
- Underbody (20 sec)
- **Focus:** Repairs, welds, paint, condition

### Interior
- Steering wheel (15 sec)
- Seats (15 sec)
- Dashboard (15 sec)
- Race modifications (20 sec if visible)

### Mechanical
- Engine (30 sec if hood open)
- Tires (10 sec)
- Suspension (20 sec if visible)

### Pit Activity
- Tyra checking car (1-2 min scattered)
- Anyone helping/prepping (30 sec)
- Tools in use (20 sec)

---

## Action Footage (During Heat)

- **Approach:** Car to course entrance (20-30 sec)
- **On-Track:** Wide shots, multiple passes if possible (1-2 min total if safe)
- **Pit Return:** Car returning, Tyra exiting (30-20 sec)
- **Post-Heat Reaction:** Handheld follow, raw emotion (1-2 min)

---

## Priority If Running Short

1. Interview audio (most important)
2. Action footage (car in race)
3. B-roll details (fill-in)
4. Community soundbites (nice-to-have)

---

## Quick Troubleshooting

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Bad audio | Re-do question, different mic position, quieter spot |
| Camera freeze | Restart, check battery, swap to backup |
| Low light | Adjust settings, phone flashlight for fill |
| Out of memory | Back up to SSD, format card |
