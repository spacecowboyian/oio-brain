# Tyra Fit Video - On-Set Notes (Bring to Shoot)

## Interview Quickstart

**Setup (10 min):** Tripod, lavalier on Tyra, car in background, test 10 sec

**Questions:**
1. What happened to your Fit?
2. How did the community help rebuild?
3. How does it feel to drive today?
4. What's next?

**Technique:** Record, ask, be quiet. Let her finish. Notes on good quotes.

---

## B-Roll Priority

**Essential (if running short):**
- Exterior (all angles)
- Car details (doors, paint, welds)
- Pit prep activity

**Nice-to-Have:**
- Interior details
- Engine (if hood open)
- Community soundbites

---

## Action Footage

**During her heat:**
- Approach to course
- On-track (if safe position)
- Pit return
- Post-heat reaction (handheld, raw)

---

## Audio Monitoring

- Headphones on during interview
- Check levels are clear and strong
- If bad audio: re-do question, different position, quieter spot

---

## Memory Card Workflow

After interview + B-roll + action block:

1. **Stop recording** → verify audio sounded good
2. **Back up** → card to SSD via USB reader
3. **Verify** → file count, play back 1 random file (10 sec)
4. **Return** → card to camera, format in-camera
5. **Test** → 10 sec recording confirms ready

---

## If Problems

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Tyra can't hear you | Mic position, quieter spot, re-do question |
| Low battery | Swap to charged battery immediately |
| Out of memory | Back up now, format card, continue |
| Camera freezes | Restart, check battery, use backup if needed |
