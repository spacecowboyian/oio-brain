# Tyra's Honda Fit Rallycross Story

## Project Overview

10-15 minute documentary-style video about Tyra's beloved Honda Fit being rebuilt as a rallycross car with community support.

**Status:** Prep complete, ready to shoot at KC Rallycross Event 3

**Solo Creator:** Ian (doing all production work)

---

## Story

T-boned Fit comes back to life thanks to community support. Follows Tyra's emotional journey rebuilding the car and competing in her first rallycross event.

---

## Shoot Details

**Event:** KC Rallycross Event 3 (confirmed)
**Build Status:** Ongoing, nearly complete (doors back on hit side)
**Target Release:** Later in 2026

### Interview (Tyra, ~30 min)
- What happened to your Fit?
- How did the community help rebuild it?
- How does it feel to drive it in a race?
- What's next for you and the car?

### B-Roll
- Car exterior (all angles, focus on repairs/welded sections)
- Interior (seats, wheel, dashboard)
- Engine (if accessible)
- Pit prep activity

### Action Footage (During her heat)
- Approach to course
- On-track footage (if safe shooting position)
- Pit lane return
- Post-heat reaction

### Optional
- Quick community soundbites (2 people max, if time)

---

## Edit Structure (10-15 min target)

1. **Opening (1-2 min):** Cold open with on-track action hook
2. **Act 1: Problem (2 min):** T-bone incident, impact, why rebuild
3. **Act 2: Community (3-4 min):** How they helped, key people, build B-roll
4. **Act 3: Race Day (2-3 min):** Pit prep, on-track, reaction
5. **Closing (1-2 min):** What's next, final shots
