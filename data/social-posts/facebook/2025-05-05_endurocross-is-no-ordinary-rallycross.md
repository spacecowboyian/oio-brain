Endurocross is no ordinary rallycross. With three laps per run and 16 total runs per car, it’s a brutal test of machine, driver, and patience. In this Kansas Region SCCA event at Mcain’s Off-Road Park in Carbondale KS, we put two cars on the opposite end of the RX spectrum to the test: Ian’s “Goblin” MR2 in Modified Rear-Wheel Drive, and Ryan’s daily-driven, heavily upgraded GD3 Honda Fit in Modified Front-Wheel Drive. Both cars were pushed hard all day across sticky mud, changing grip conditions, and 2 different courses.

What started as a last minute decision to run the MR2 turned into a full day of reliable performance. Meanwhile, the Fit continued to prove why it’s one of the most underrated motorsports platforms out there. With co-drivers in each car, temperamental power plants and a rerun redemption arc, this event could be the perfect showcase of grassroots motorsport durability, or a perfect guide to "how to kill your race car".
