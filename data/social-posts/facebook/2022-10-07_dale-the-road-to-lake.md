Dale: The road to Lake Garnett Grand Prix Revival

Today was all carb. Erik from Hot Hatch Racing came over and helped me out with swapping in bigger jets and air correctors in the weber 32/36 and what do you know.. Dale now runs rich. :D He also noted that the fuel in the bowl smelled terrible and had a good deal of sediment in it and this brought about the theory that we are still washing really old fuel out of the tank even after 4 fillups that were all done at about a half a tank.. even though Dale said he was empty.

We also, along the way, lost most of the gauges on the dash to some electrical gremlin. I now have no tach, water temp, oil pressure or fuel level gauges. Pretty fun.

I decided to wash him up this evening and the results of him losing 15 years of barn dust are pictured here next to the #rxfx that also got a bit of a hose down. (Extra points if you can spot the pit crew member in the photo) Out at dinner I messaged the fine folks at the #LGGPR and asked them to downgrade me to the 60mph touring class. It's the right, proper and safe thing to do since dale has seen exactly ZERO track time under my stewardship. So I'll cruise him around and smile and wave for the crowd. I think he'll be well loved even at a slower speed.

But once we came home we tackled the issue of dale not getting enough fuel.. even though he was running rich :). So we tried a clear fuel filter and the fuel was just trickling in. This led the crew to believe that the amazon fuel pump I bought was the problem.. We took it off and cleaned it out as best we could. Hooked it back up and.. same old dale. Went out to oreilly and bought a brand new unit.. hooked it up.. same dale.

On the way back from that last drive as I repeated to myself "how could he run rich.. without getting enough gas.." it finally dawned on me. TIMING YOU IDIOT! He's not burning that gas properly. So, very scientifically, I undid the bolt on the dizzy and gave it a little advance. Tightened it back down and blipped the throttle.. it came up quite quickly.

Got in the car and no sooner than I put it in reverse and touched the gas.. I knew.. dale had much more life than before.

So there's still some fine tuning to do. Tomorrow AM we will swap back to the original jets and air correctors to lean him back out. Perhaps actually break out the timing light and see how far advanced he wants to be. Replace a burnt out headlight. Replace a broken drivers side window crank. Jiggle wires everywhere to see if the gauges on the dash will come back... then load him on the trailer (after adding back the tow hooks in the front) and drag his butt down to Garnett!
